How I work

Four problems I worked through, told as situation, task, action and result.

Turning hard shifts into easier ones

McDonald's and Nando's, 2022 to now

Situation
Kiosks, POS terminals, printers and kitchen screens failed in the middle of rushes and the crew didn't know what to try. Rosters were thin or changed at the last minute, new starters needed constant help, and queues backed up.
Task
As shift leader and then shift manager, make busy shifts run well enough that the team could keep serving even when something went wrong.
Action
I wrote a short "try this first" checklist for the faults we saw most, so the crew could clear the basics before calling a vendor. I set up a proper way to train and buddy new starters, planned rosters and rush positions ahead of time, and briefed the team before each rush and kept them updated while anything was down.
Result
Faults were fixed faster with fewer vendor calls, service moved faster, new crew stood on their own sooner, and my manager noticed the difference.

Video outreach that checks itself

IT and Automation Intern, 2026

Situation
Outreach videos were started by typing each prospect's details into a Google Sheet by hand, and weak prospects got videos too.
Task
Let anyone start a video from a chat, and stop spending effort on prospects whose websites weren't a good fit.
Action
I made the AI assistant the starting point. It asks for 5 details, checks the video template exists and scores the website from 1 to 10. Below 7 it stops and writes down why. At 7 or above it creates the video through the RepliQ API and writes the video details back to the same row. It only runs when someone asks.
Result
No more typing into the spreadsheet, weak prospects filtered out automatically, and every request recorded in one place.

Finding which companies visit a website

IT and Automation Intern, 2026

Situation
The business could see visits to its website but not which companies they came from.
Task
Identify visiting companies automatically and save them as possible customers.
Action
I adapted an n8n workflow. Google Tag Manager passes each visit to n8n, IPinfo looks up who owns the connection, Apify collects public company details, and the result goes to Airtable and the CRM. Most lookups came back as home and mobile providers, so I worked on filtering those out, since every lookup costs money.
Result
A working visitor-to-lead process, and a useful lesson for the business: most website traffic is personal, so it needs filtering first.

Writing guides people can follow

IT and Automation Intern, 2026

Situation
Interns learned tools by asking each other, so the same questions kept coming up.
Task
Make each process repeatable by someone who hadn't done it before.
Action
After every task I wrote a step-by-step guide and recorded a short video, linked to the task in Airtable. For technical tools I covered start-up, settings, health checks, updates and reading the logs.
Result
15 guides finished or approved, including the daily workflow every intern follows. It's the same skill as writing knowledge-base articles for a service desk.

Privacy

Last updated 7 November 2026

This is my personal portfolio. It has no accounts, no forms and no advertising, and it does not sell or share anything about you.

What the site stores in your browser

To make the site work smoothly, your browser keeps a few small notes for this visit only (in session storage). They are deleted when you close the tab and are never sent anywhere:

  • your conversation with the site helper (the eye in the corner), so it carries on as you change pages;
  • where you dragged the eye to, and whether its greeting has been shown;
  • which one-time animations have already played.

The site does not use cookies.

Analytics

No analytics or tracking are running on this site at the moment. If I add visitor statistics, I will use a privacy-friendly, cookie-free service and update this page first.

Fonts, video and links

The fonts, images and intro video are all served from this site, so no outside service sees your visit. Links to LinkedIn, Coursera, Skillsoft and other sites take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies. The email links open your own mail app.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address to keep the service secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
